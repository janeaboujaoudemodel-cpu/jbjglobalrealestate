## 6. Link-earning ground rules (non-negotiable when negotiating placements)

- **Every** placement must link `https://www.jbj.ae/insights/future-of-real-estate-2026` (the pillar) — the homepage is fine as a *second* link but never the only one.
- Byline links must be `<a href>` in the article body or author bio, not just `jbj.ae` in plain text.
- Reject `rel="sponsored"` and `rel="nofollow"` — offer to swap for an unbranded data credit if the desk insists on nofollow.
- Never accept "advertorial" framing — it kills the SEO value and burns editorial trust for the next round.
