## What to AVOID (competitor traps to not repeat)

Semrush shows fam-properties and providentestate get flooded with these — **do not** replicate:

- ❌ Blogspot / WordPress.com auto-generated backlink farms (`*.blogspot.com`, `cheap-dubai-property-investment.blogspot.com`)
- ❌ Telegram link-seller anchors (`tg @bhs_links`, `@seo_anomaly`, `@links_dealer`) — these are Google-penalized paid link schemes
- ❌ Generic directory farms (`allwebsitesdirectory.com`, `bestwebstats.com`, `domainsc.com`, `factmags.com`)
- ❌ Comment-spam links on Korean/Chinese unrelated sites (fam-properties' top anchor `포항출장만남` is Korean escort spam)
- ❌ Fiverr "1000 backlinks for $5" gigs — already showing up in our own tiny profile (`fiverr-seo-for-small-businesses.site`). Disavow these in GSC.
