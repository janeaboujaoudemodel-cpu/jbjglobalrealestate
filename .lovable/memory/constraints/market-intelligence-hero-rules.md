The `/market-intelligence` hero must contain ONLY: the `Market Intelligence` h1, the description paragraph, and the two CTAs ("Explore Market Dashboard", "View Daily Reports"). Never re-add: JBJ logo, "Daily Market Desk" eyebrow, animated keyword marquee (DLD/RERA/DXB/TRANSACTIONS/…), source badge row (DLD/RERA/DXB Interact/Dubai Statistics/JBJ Intelligence), or the "Founder Market Intelligence" card. Champagne overlay must stay opaque (≥0.94) so the hero never looks faded.

Number markers on all Market Intelligence pages (Area Intelligence, Methodology, etc.) must use solid champagne `bg-[#EFE6D6]` + `border-[#B89555]` + gold/ink numeral. Never `bg-[#EFE6D6]/20` (renders as a blank/white circle).
