name: Market Intelligence hero rules
description: MI hero must stay champagne with only title/description/CTAs; no logo, eyebrow, badges, keyword marquee, founder card, or faded overlay
type: constraint
---
The `/market-intelligence` hero must contain ONLY: the `Market Intelligence` h1, the description paragraph, and the two CTAs ("Explore Market Dashboard", "View Daily Reports"). Never re-add: JBJ logo, "Daily Market Desk" eyebrow, animated keyword marquee (DLD/RERA/DXB/TRANSACTIONS/…), source badge row (DLD/RERA/DXB Interact/Dubai Statistics/JBJ Intelligence), or the "Founder Market Intelligence" card. The hero must be full-screen on every device (`.jj-hero-fullscreen`) with visible video/image media and only a light legibility overlay; never use a heavy champagne/white wash that makes the media look faded.

All Market Intelligence subpage heroes (`/overview`, `/areas`, `/reports`, `/methodology`) must use the same full-screen video/photo hero behavior with reduced fade and high-contrast white copy over media.

Number markers on all Market Intelligence pages (Area Intelligence, Methodology, etc.) must use solid champagne `bg-[#EFE6D6]` + `border-[#B89555]` + gold/ink numeral. Never `bg-[#EFE6D6]/20` (renders as a blank/white circle).
