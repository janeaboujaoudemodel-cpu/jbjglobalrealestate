---
name: No Gold Borders on Emerald
description: Emerald surfaces, cards, pills, filters, and buttons must never use gold/champagne borders or divider lines.
type: constraint
---
Never place gold/champagne borders, hairlines, or divider strokes around emerald/dark-green UI surfaces.

Use white translucent hairlines (`rgba(255,255,255,...)`) or deeper/lighter emerald strokes on emerald components instead.

Gold/champagne may be used as a background surface, but not as the border/divider around emerald buttons, cards, filter bars, or CTA panels.

Why: the owner explicitly rejected gold borders around emerald as a visual violation.
